# Design

Preserve Vite/TypeScript and the upgraded lockfile. Add explicit zh-CN/en locale registration and separate content collections; keep stable chapter and research IDs. Locale switching remounts the current route while preserving theme and chapter completion. New locales are enabled only with content bundles, avoiding silent mixed-language fallback.

Root owns app shell, theme tokens, routing, UI localization, learning widgets, resource/figure libraries, assets, export and integration. Two agents translate complete foundation/advanced chapter bodies and guides into new English-only content files without touching originals. One agent researches and authors the bilingual multi-page research collection and evidence matrix. Shared data contracts are specified in delegation.

Generated bitmap illustrations communicate conceptual components; exact connectivity and equations remain in Mermaid and accessible textual explanations. Figure records carry category, keywords, localized explanation, provenance and source-page link. References use one normalized catalogue with topic/evidence filters and safe wrapping.

Remove notes/bookmarks/resume controls and handlers. Retain only relevant local preferences and chapter completion; ignore obsolete stored fields without a destructive migration of unrelated browser data. No live equipment, new heavy frameworks, API credentials, remote translation dependency, or public hosting changes.
