# Teaching and interface design

Keep the visual system, assets, 24 chapters and 14 focused labs. Add three connected layers: mathematical preparation, a cement-kiln decision workbench, and a structured guided derivation in every chapter. The flow is situation → symbols → one reasoning step at a time → numerical substitution → explanatory checkpoint → formal chapter and focused lab.

Guides use src/learning-types.ts and pure data files, allowing the same material to be rendered interactively and exported in full. Formula fields are raw LaTeX; narrative fields may contain Markdown. Checkpoints explain every answer choice. Industrial process semantics remain supervisory bounded setpoint increments, not direct actuator commands.

Keep private personal context out of public source; use only the research-domain connections needed to teach. Any cited paper's authorship must be verified from a primary source. Mark project contracts as project-specific and simulator dynamics as educational assumptions.
